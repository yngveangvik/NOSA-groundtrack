    About this display

    Positions and ground tracks are calculated with SGP4 using CelesTrak orbital elements. Speed is the magnitude of the SGP4 velocity in the TEME reference frame. The daylight boundary follows the Sun at the displayed UTC time.

    Predicted passes indicate geometric visibility above 10° elevation at the selected station. Orbital elements are refreshed every two hours when available. Cached elements support offline calculation.
